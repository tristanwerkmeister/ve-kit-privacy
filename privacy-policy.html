VE Kit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Published copy: github.com/tristanwerkmeister/ve-kit-privacy (synced from the private VE Kit repo; Chrome Web Store uses GitHub Pages).

VE Kit is a browser extension for CNN Video Editorial staff. It works only on pages you already have access to, and formats information shown there so you can copy it or prepare an email draft:

  • Humanity (When I Work) — reads the schedule shown in List View and formats daily shift lists and weekly staffing notes.
  • TikTok Studio (tiktok.com/tiktokstudio/content) — reads the post captions, publish times, view counts and post links shown in your Posts list, and lists the three most-viewed posts for a day you choose.
  • Domo (cnn.domo.com) — on the Top Vertical Videos card, requests that card's video titles and Video Starts from Domo using your existing signed-in session (the same request the Domo page makes to draw the card), and lists the top three.
  • VN affiliate content (vn.turner.com/VN/viewAffiliateVideo.jsp) — reads the Status column of the affiliate list and hides rows whose status you have not ticked.
  • StoryHub (Airtable, airtable.com) — on a content record, reads the headline, people and description shown there, and when you click its buttons writes into the record's description for you: the release note, the handover note and a link to the record's script. It also fills in Airtable's Add Content form when you click Create VV or 16x9. These edits are made in your browser, as you, exactly as if you had typed them.
  • Stellar (cms.cnn.com) — when you click Shared component in StoryHub, opens Stellar and fills in a new video resource with the headline, description, CNN ID and the photos you picked.
  • SharePoint (warnermedia.sharepoint.com, the VEScripts site) — when you click Create script, copies the team's Word script template into that day's Daily Scripts folder, named after the record's headline, and opens it.
  • Outlook (outlook.office.com, or your default mail app) — when you click Send to FC in StoryHub, opens a new email draft to the fact-check team with the record's headline, its script link and your first name, and copies the same text to your clipboard. You review and send it yourself.

Data collection

  • VE Kit does not send schedule, video or analytics data to the developer or to any third-party server, and does not use analytics or tracking.
  • Processing happens locally in your browser on the tab you are using. VE Kit makes network requests only to the services you already use, using your existing signed-in session, and nowhere else:
    • Domo — the Top Vertical Videos card request above.
    • SharePoint — for Create script: it checks that day's Daily Scripts folder, checks whether a script with that name already exists, copies the template there and reads the new file's link. These requests act with your own SharePoint permissions, so the new file shows you as its author. To make them, VE Kit may briefly open a VEScripts page in a background tab and close it again, or ask you to sign in to SharePoint in a new tab.
  • Video lists from TikTok Studio and Domo are held in memory for the open page only and are not saved.
  • Optional Humanity panel settings (output mode, toggles, panel collapsed/expanded), the statuses ticked in the VN affiliate filter, the name and StoryHub settings you enter on the welcome or settings page, your Send to FC email wording, when you accepted this policy, each StoryHub record's progress and script link, and the photos you pick for a Stellar thumbnail are stored with Chrome extension storage on your device only.
  • VE Kit uses Chrome's debugger permission for one thing: to click Save in Stellar's Image Settings when it uploads a thumbnail for you, because that button only responds to a real mouse click. Chrome shows a "debugging this browser" bar while it does. It reads nothing through the debugger and only acts on the Stellar tab it is filling in.
  • VE Kit keeps a debug log of what it does on StoryHub and Stellar (for example headlines, release note text, names shown on the record and the page markup it was working with) in Chrome extension storage on your device, for up to 7 days. It is never sent anywhere automatically. You can download, copy or clear it from the VE Kit settings page, and choose whether to share it with whoever supports VE Kit for you.
  • Information leaves the page only when you choose to: by clicking Copy (to your clipboard), by downloading an email draft (.eml) to your computer, or by opening an email draft with Send to FC. VE Kit never sends email or posts anything for you.

Permissions

  • Site access — Humanity (humanity.com), TikTok Studio's Posts page (tiktok.com/tiktokstudio/content), Domo (cnn.domo.com), the VN affiliate content list (vn.turner.com/VN/viewAffiliateVideo.jsp), StoryHub (airtable.com) and Stellar (cms.cnn.com), so VE Kit can show its panels and buttons and read those pages; SharePoint (warnermedia.sharepoint.com) for Create script.
  • activeTab / scripting — show or hide VE Kit on the open supported page when you click the extension icon, and run Create script's SharePoint requests inside a VEScripts page.
  • debugger — click Save in Stellar's Image Settings, as described above.
  • clipboardWrite — copy the formatted roster, note or video list when you click Copy.
  • storage / unlimitedStorage — remember the settings and data listed above on this device; unlimited storage is needed only for the thumbnail photos, which can be large.
