Shifty Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Published copy: github.com/tristanwerkmeister/shifty-privacy (synced from the private Shifty repo; Chrome Web Store uses GitHub Pages).

Shifty is a browser extension for CNN Video Editorial staff. It reads schedule information displayed on Humanity (When I Work) pages that you already have access to, and formats that information for copy or email drafts.

Data collection

  • Shifty does not send schedule data to the developer or to third-party analytics servers.
  • Processing happens locally in your browser on the active Humanity tab.
  • Optional settings (output mode, toggles, dates) are stored with Chrome extension storage on your device only.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — read the open Humanity schedule page when you use Shifty.
  • clipboardWrite — copy formatted roster text when you choose Copy.
  • storage — remember your panel preferences on this device.
